## Dispatch-Discipline Mechanisms

(per debt-paydown synthesis [PR #810](https://github.com/gunb-ai/gunbc/pull/810) §4) — three layered mechanisms convert P5 from a steady-state principle into reviewable per-event gates.

- **(a) Paired-dispatch at brief-authoring time**: every ad-hoc worker brief that introduces a scaffold (any new hand-Rust file, any new sentinel, any new Rust mirror of a `.dag` authority) MUST name in its "Acceptance" section: the dissolution trigger; the adjacent debt row in [`_internal/ROADMAP_OPS.md`](ROADMAP_OPS.md) it touches; whether it contributes to or defers from that row's dissolution. If the brief introduces a string/path/name identity bridge (sentinel, fixture-name routing, `span.file ==` check, `include_str!` side-channel), it MUST be authored against the §0 identity-carrier pass program, not as a one-off.
- **(b) Per-PR gate (the early warning)**: no new or expanded hand-Rust under `src/v3/` lands without the PR description stating **exactly one** checkable receipt: a deleted file/scaffold path, an SG-0 census line shrink with before/after counts in `sg0_census_test.rs`, or an explicit deferral that names a lane **and** cites a concrete [`_internal/ROADMAP_OPS.md`](ROADMAP_OPS.md) row (path + heading/anchor or permalink). Vague deferrals ("see ROADMAP", "TBD", narrative without a cited row) do not satisfy the gate. **Hand-Rust includes Rust tests** (`src/v3/compiler/tests/**`) — these are the T-PB-B test subset of the SG-0 census; the gate applies the same way it applies to T-PB-A non-test files. Author-facing checklist: [`.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`](../.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md).
- **(c) Velocity tripwire (the late warning)**: cadence pass (see [`_internal/ROADMAP_OPS.md`](ROADMAP_OPS.md) §"Integration-reflection cadence") reports introduction:dissolution ratio for the window; ≥3:1 in any 7-day window puts ad-hoc lane dispatch under Director review until the ratio recovers, after a manual sweep for dissolution-bearing feature PRs to avoid heuristic false positives. Below 3:1, advisory only.

The three mechanisms compose: per-PR gate is finest grain (every PR); paired-dispatch is brief-authoring grain; tripwire is window grain.

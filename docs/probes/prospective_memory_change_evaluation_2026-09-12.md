# Prospective memory-change assessment

**Goal:** withhold production admission when a proposed positioned population or any initialization step conflicts with evidence or lacks a mandatory answer.

**Method:** `gunbc.host.memory_configuration.MemoryChangeProposal` binds initial/proposed populations, platform/firmware/settings, operating questions and actions. `memory_configuration_evaluation` joins bounded observations, catalog authorities and independent qualification through `GoalAssessment`; `memory_change_evaluation` assesses initialization snapshots. `product.build_fulfillment.BuildResolution` carries the resulting purpose, assessment and ceiling once into selection. Retained modules are checked without becoming purchases.

**Invariants:** required identities correspond exactly to unique results; empty or unsupported obligation lists cannot admit. Placement derives from documented connector roles and refuses missing/ambiguous joins. Delivery alternatives remain bound; possible conflicts require supplier restriction, receiving inspection or qualification rather than claiming undelivered hardware failed. Powered-off mixtures differ from initialization. Qualification retains its population bound and completion obligations, never becoming production readiness through an unfillable line, positive market standing or `PartNotImplicated`.

**Evidence:** `gunbc.host.memory_observation.read_verified_memory_capture` hashes the same bytes it returns to recognition. Collector coverage includes process result, stimulus, interval and positioned inventory; repeated hashes alone do not prove self-observation. `mtcollins_training_mixture_finding` owns the firmware-specific recognizer and bindings; generic validation invents no SKU suffix, coordinate convention or population size. The observed byte pair is not a catalog-derived expectation and stays scoped to its captured attempt. Uniformity answers only the bounded mixing question, not class/part qualification, training, boot or another socket.

**Remaining consumers:** `memory_change_consumer_frontiers` names the absent order actuator and physical convergence. They must consume exact evidence-bound admission and independently reread actual identities, bindings, placement and settings before reassessment. `PopulationOption` is frozen for host answers; `product.node_power_envelope` still needs a positioned mixed-population model and atomic retirement of its aggregate input. Data-rate obligations remain missing-model; uncited 2DPC ceilings are not constraints.

**Validate:** run `assess_mtcollins_memory_change` in `dag/gunbc/host/mtcollins_memory_change.dag` with source roots `dag` and `src/v2`; `explain_mtcollins_memory_change` exposes the structured explanation. This replays committed evidence and expects production refusal, not hardware actuation. `test.claim.host.memory_change_evaluation_witness` and fulfillment/selection/digest controls cover malformed coverage, replacement bytes, collector refusal and carried ceilings. Historical wet results and mutation controls are in the linked account; hermetic `NoMockResponse` and pre-evaluation refusal are unevaluated. They do not substitute for current required CI.

## What re-deriving changed, stated as differences from the inherited boards

1. **T7 is GONE at this subject.** The 2026-08-18 board's largest E0308 root — seed-prelude `Hash`
   collision, `Fnv1a64Structural` ↔ `String`, 99 sites / 24.3% at M=11 — has **zero sites** here.
   No attribution is offered (§16): this is M=1 against that board's M=11, and a root that lived
   mostly outside 03_ingest's closure would read as zero here without anything having been fixed.
   **The claim is about this subject, not about the corpus.**
2. **The largest live root is B3, not T7 or R1**, and its shape is specific: 28 of its 49 sites are
   an integer *literal* (`48`, `57`, `0`…`9`) at a `Rc<Nat>` parameter, 18 an `i64`-realized value.
3. **`RT-builtin` (20) is a category the older partitions had no row for.** The discriminator is
   mechanical and in the data: `note: function defined here → src/v1_rt.rs`. It is the E0308 face
   of the bare-name interception root already characterized on the E0061/E0282 side (`contains`,
   `lookup`) — the emitter resolves a call to a host builtin that merely *shares a spelling* with
   the corpus declaration, and the builtin's signature is `String`-typed.
4. **`ARG-ORDER` (11) is new and is not a carrier fork at all.** rustc's own `reorder these
   arguments` fires on `eval_transform_node` / `eval_branch_node` / `eval_loop_node` /
   `eval_bind_node` / `eval_match_node` in `v2_compiler_eval.rs`: the emitted call passes
   `(node, args, tree, interpretation, …)` against a declaration reading
   `(node, _args, _facts, tree, interpreter, …)`. This is the failure DESIGN.md §4b names when it
   says the emitter "reorders a mislabeled call into positional leftovers" — here it is measured,
   with 11 sites and five named callees.
5. **`A-clone` (6) shows Root A surfacing under E0308**, not only E0277. Its signature is
   `expected type parameter `T`, found `&T`` inside a generated variant accessor
   (`FidelityDisposition::feature`, `PassingCandidateFold::candidate`, `ListHeadResult::value`,
   `CacheProbe::value`, …). An E0277-only view of Root A misses these.
6. **`R1` splits by depth.** 11 of its 33 sites differ only by an `Rc` *inside a type argument*
   (`Rc<Measure<(), S, Rc<i64>>>` vs `Rc<Measure<(), S, i64>>`). A wrap-decision fix that only
   normalizes the outer position leaves a third of R1 standing.

all four — §11.21's field/signature refutation is the standing warning against exactly that move.

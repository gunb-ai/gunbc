## Root split (how much is one root vs independent tails)

Two primary roots explain essentially the whole 228. Parent's prior (repr-straddle /
Rc↔T / native↔modeled) is **directionally right**; the census sharpens it into two
named mechanisms.

### Root R1 — PartialEq / binop over `Rc<_>` that is not `PartialEq` / not arithmetic

**116 instances (50.9% of 228)** — complementary to R2's 112; every instance is in exactly one slice below.

Two realization surfaces of the **same** Rc-straddle:

| sub-root | count | what fails | emitted evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **R1a-derive** | 68 | `#[derive(..., PartialEq, ...)]` expands `==` over fields that are `Rc<im::Vector<_>>`, `Rc<HashMap<...>>`, `Rc<*Interpreter>` | rustc points at the derive line on `RuntimeBehaviorInterpreter`, collection carriers, etc. (`v2_std_runtime.rs`, `v2_std_algebra.rs`, `v2_std_collection.rs`, `v2_compiler_infer.rs`) |

| **R1b-Nat** | 18 (3×6 modules; emit_host spells `Rc<v2_std_nat::Nat>`) | `<` and `/` on `Rc<Nat>` (no `PartialOrd`/`Div` on the Rc wrapper) | `if (a.clone() < b.clone())` in `v2_std_nat.rs:58`; `value.clone() < 10` / `/ 10` in `v2_std_integer.rs:1407–1411` |

## Explicit one-root vs tails accounting

Fail-closed partition of all 228 (each instance in exactly one row; sums to 228).
Verified against `shapes/*.instances.tsv` — the dyn-Fn / InterpretationAlgebra /
EffectIo multiset is **identical** in `05_eval` and `emit_host` (15 each), not eval-only.

| slice | instances | % of 228 | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| R1a-derive | 68 | 29.8% | PartialEq derive over non-eq `Rc<_>` fields |
| R1a-expr (dyn Fn / InterpretationAlgebra / EffectIo) | 30 | 13.2% | **05_eval 15 + emit_host 15** (identical multiset; same binop site) |
| R1b-Nat | 18 | 7.9% | `<`/`/` on `Rc<Nat>` (3×6 modules; emit_host path-spells `Rc<v2_std_nat::Nat>`) |
| emit_host R2 (Measure/Semiring, incl. double-Rc) | 52 | 22.8% | **R2** |
| materialization_carriers R2 | 60 | 26.3% | **R2** (same family, single-Rc) |
| **total** | **228** | **100%** | R1 = 68+30+18 = **116**; R2 = 52+60 = **112** |

Flat-14 geometry (same root, different cut): the shared 12-shape / 14-instance
multiset × 4 modules = 56, plus 27 flat-msg residues in 05_eval (14) + emit_host (11)
+ mat (2 Vector) = 83 of the 116 R1 instances; the remaining 33 R1 are the 30-instance
R1a-expr tail and the 3 emit_host-only `Rc<v2_std_nat::Nat>` R1b spellings outside the
flat message text.

**Bottom line:** 116/228 (50.9%) is **R1** (PartialEq derive + raw binops over
non-PartialEq / non-arithmetic `Rc<_>`), 112/228 (49.1%) is **R2** (Measure/Semiring under
Rc, concentrated in emit_host + materialization_carriers). The flat-14 signal is
entirely R1. There is **no third large independent root** at this grain.

## Method notes / known parser limits

- Operand left/right extraction from rustc notes is incomplete when the diagnostic is
  the single-type form (`cannot be applied to type \`T\``); the `single` column and
  message text carry the type in those cases.
- The "code" column often shows the `#[derive(...)]` line for R1a-derive (correct —
  that is where rustc locates the expanded `==`).
- Span evidence is in `shapes/*.instances.tsv` (`path`/`line`/`col`/`code`); emitted
  crate trees were probe-local and are not banked (re-emit via the stamp protocol if
  a span file must be re-opened).

THE HASH IS THE ANCHOR, NOT THE COMMIT LINE. A commit cannot contain its own id,
so any stamp written into a tracked file names an ancestor by construction; the
commit above is the closest one at write time and later docs-only commits do not
move the corpus. Verify the anchor against whatever HEAD you have, rather than
trusting either line:

  find dag src/v2 -name '*.dag' -print0 | sort -z | xargs -0 sha256sum | sha256sum

Equal to the hash above means the receipts in this directory describe your tree,
whatever the commit line says. Different means they do not, and the reproducer in
../README.md regenerates them.

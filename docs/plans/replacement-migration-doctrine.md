# Replacement migration doctrine: cut over at the root

*Provenance: this doctrine was carried inline in `DESIGN.md` §3 until 2026-08-18, when it was extracted on operator ruling — `DESIGN.md` is a first-principles document, and a 4,400-word procedure with its receipts is doctrine rather than principle. The text is preserved verbatim. `DESIGN.md` §3 states the principle and links here; this file is the authority for the procedure, the carve-outs, the disposition census, the terminal receipt, and the dated receipts on both sides.*

### §3 standing rule — replacement migrations cut over at the root (operator ruling, 2026-08-15)

When two structures answer the same semantic question and one is intended to disappear, the change is a **replacement migration**, not an ordinary refinement. A replacement is not performed by gradually editing the old authority from its leaves upward until it resembles the new one. That approach preserves the old root while every intermediate state becomes observable and load-bearing: `X`, partial `Y`, adapters between them, comparison machinery, fallback arms, and consumers that understand both. Each intermediate state then acquires its own projections, witnesses, enrollment, compatibility decisions and eventual deletion — so the migration creates more authority before it removes any. The intuitive error is pruning a weed from the top while leaving its root in the ground: every cut changes the visible shape while preserving the mechanism that makes the unwanted thing return. And the ruthless half, ruled from live receipts (operator, 2026-08-15): **uproot the weed as early as you can, and fix forward minimally with the new solution** — because a surviving X is an *attractor*. Every agent session that enters the lane inherits its decomposition, studies its structure, unions its rosters, and preserves its parts, since deleting piece-by-piece requires first proving nothing depends on each piece; the weed's structure recruits gardeners. Worse, tip-first deletion is self-defeating by construction: every intermediate state must keep X alive and green, so the deletion program degenerates into maintenance — and then optimization — of the thing being deleted; you pay to improve what you are removing. Starting from the empty replacement inverts the burden of proof: nothing is in Y unless someone put it there, the denominator is explicit, nothing is carried because no one dared remove it, and nothing can newly depend on a thing that is gone. And the census itself is often the herring — measuring a structure you are about to delete is the attractor at work, enumerating what X *says* it provides instead of what is actually consumed. In a fail-closed substrate the **deletion is the census**: every real dependent refuses loudly — compile errors, unresolved references, red witnesses — so what breaks is exactly what was load-bearing ("load-bearing" is not intrinsic to a node; it is precisely the obligations that surface when the node is gone), and the deleter owns designing and fixing forward everything that actually breaks, until the tree is green again. What cannot break loudly — un-enrolled evidence, external consumers, coverage that was already silent — is the one thing deletion cannot discover, and it is covered not by a census but by the declared, bounded §4b rung-drop (previous rung, temporary rung, reason, restoration trigger — loud in the commit, never silent). gunbc#8146 is this shape merged: most of the old suite carried no load at all, and its deletion needed no solving. This follows from the sections above rather than being layered onto them — §2 prices an intermediate representation destined to be discarded as redundant work, §3 forbids X and Y both answering for one fact, and §5 rules that naming a dissolution condition does not authorize introducing the adapters and fallbacks that constitute the dual-authority period.

The operative loop, compressed (operator sequence, 2026-08-15):

```
1. back up to the root of the weed and delete it
     -> the tree immediately surfaces both censuses:
        the loads        (what depended on X)
        the concealments (what X was hiding)

2. for each literal compile error, solve from first principles:
        implement the terminal solution in Y
      | migrate the corpus (X as offline oracle)
      | retire the need, with a receipt
      | the error names another weed -> recurse to 1

3. stop when the tree is green
   and the declared silent residue is restored or retired
```

**What deferring the deletion costs** (the consequence catalogue, operator-directed 2026-08-15): while X stands, every nearby question is answered in X's vocabulary — the only operational terms present — so the organization is continuously forced into decisions premised on assumptions already scheduled for death. Four consequences, each strictly worse than the last: (1) **waste** — improvements to the thing being replaced die with it; (2) **propagation** — each dead-premise decision becomes a premise for the next: an optimized X's caches, schedules and vocabulary leak into Y's design space, so Y ends up negotiating with X's optimizations, and the dead assumption recruits dependents — removable debt converted into architectural constraint; (3) **evidence inversion** — an optimized X looks healthier, so each improvement reads as evidence the replacement is less urgent: §5's absorbing fallback operating at the architecture grain, the deficit's observed frequency zeroed by the very work that deepens it; (4) **unmarked premises** — a decision carries no tag naming the doomed assumption it rests on, so the eventual migration pays an archaeology cost to find and reverse premise-contaminated decisions — §1's deferred cost at interest, the stale-citation class generalized from prose citations to design decisions. The receipts paragraph below measures all four on the two bankrupted branches: the import-era completion and the coordinator re-fit are (1) and (2) caught live; that both branches read as progress while deepening the deficit is (3); the recut each then required is (4)'s archaeology, paid. Beneath all four is the axiom: A3 grounds agreement only on what stays stable across time, and a dead assumption is definitionally time-unstable — building on one is citing an authority already known to be revoked. This is also the frozen-X carve-out's real function: where X must keep running because no Y can hold the boundary, the freeze is not thrift — it **caps the premise supply**, so no new decision is grounded on the dying design. The same mechanism run forward is why delete-first needs no ongoing enforcement once performed (operator, 2026-08-15): with X gone its vocabulary is unavailable, so the cheapest next action and the first-principles action coincide — the existing approach stops being reachable, and solving the problem normally becomes the only move left. Hence the standing exercise that opens every cut: enumerate what is certainly scheduled for death already — the old pipeline step, the superseded grammar, the machinery only X consumes — and delete those files immediately, before any fix-forward work, so no further decision can be premised on them.

**The census is EMPTY for any referent that is named rather than imported** (measured 2026-08-22; the floor cut that deleted the workflows is gunbc#8283). Delete-first rests on the deletion *being* the census: every real dependent refuses loudly, so what breaks is exactly what was load-bearing. That mechanism reaches exactly as far as the dependency graph does. A carrier that *names* the deleted thing in prose — rather than importing it — has no edge to refuse along, so it survives the cut asserting, in the present tense, that the deleted thing still runs. This is a fourth member of the "cannot break loudly" set above, and it is worse than the other three: un-enrolled evidence and silent coverage are *absences* a reader can suspect, while a stale prose claim is a *positive assertion* that reads as documentation and is believed.

**Workflow files are currently such a referent, and the measurement is on the record.** The floor cut deleted `ci.yml`, `falsifier.yml` and `falsifier-alert.yml`. Nothing refused. As of 2026-08-22 `ci.yml` is still named **99 times across 39 `.dag` files** — 21 production carriers, 11 under `gunbc/plans`, 7 witnesses; the two falsifier workflows appear in 12 more. **Read the two numbers as different kinds of thing:** 99 is a measured upper bound (every occurrence, including legitimately historical ones and carriers describing a workflow's *emission* rather than its cadence); a text heuristic over production carriers whose surrounding prose says "runs on / executes / cadence / required ci" narrows the likely-misleading set to **7**, and that 7 is a *hypothesis about prose that only a reader can confirm*, not a defect count. Anyone citing 7 as a measurement is citing a grep's opinion about English.

The live specimen that produced this row: `gunbc.ci_spec` `gunbc_ci_collect_fleet_convergence_invoke` still read that it was "the LIVE invocation wire … runs on main pushes downstream of the required ci check, from srv1 … executes on a real cadence." It has zero consumers and built a step for the deleted workflow. A session read it before the code, found the code consistent with it, and nearly reported a live actuation defect — SSH against two rack positions with no chassis — that does not occur. Only enumerating the surviving workflows' actual `--entry` points separated the claim from the fact. Cost: real session time, and a near-miss on a false finding reaching an operator.

**A sweep has already been run once for the adjacent class, and it recurred** — which is the receipt for the ruling below rather than an argument for it. gunbc#8774 (2026-08-21) repaired 23 of 27 dangling cited-symbol references "the cuts left behind", and its own title says plainly that the lens which should have caught them still runs nowhere. That pass was one day before this measurement, and it did not touch the prose-named-workflow population at all — because a cited-*symbol* lens is exactly the mechanism that cannot see a referent which is not a symbol. Two different classes, one root: the corpus can check what it can resolve, and a filename in a comment is not resolvable.

**The fix is not a sweep** (operator ruling, 2026-08-22). Correcting seven sentences fixes seven sentences and leaves the class exactly as open; the next workflow deleted decays identically and someone repeats the count. A sweep that must be re-run after every cut is a subscription, not a fix. **Next-rung trigger — and the mechanism already exists in-tree, verified rather than proposed:** a workflow's path is *already* a symbol. `gunbc.generated_artifact` carries `WitnessFloorYamlArtifact => ".github/workflows/witnesses.yml"` as a variant of a closed coproduct, and `gunbc.witness_floor_workflow` exports `witness_floor_workflow_name` beside it. So the durable form is that a carrier discussing a workflow references **the artifact variant**, never the filename spelled in prose — and because the variant belongs to a closed coproduct, deleting it forces every `match` over that type to stop compiling. The census then runs itself: exhaustiveness *is* the refusal. That moves the class from *mitigatable* (prose-hygiene diligence, which is exactly what it is today, and what the §4b rung honestly is) to *structurally guaranteed* for any carrier that adopts it — stronger than the merely-preventable lens this document's sibling class is still waiting on, because no lens has to run. Until that lands, the obligation on anyone deleting a prose-named subject is explicit and manual: **grep the deleted name across the corpus in the same change**, because the compiler will not.

**Existing designs are not substitutes for deletion** (operator ruling, 2026-08-15). A design document, plan carrier, or staged roadmap authored while X lived is itself one of the premise-contaminated decisions above: its terminal shape, area maps, and named obligations may be valid evidence, but its *sequencing* — hollow gradually, migrate then delete, delete only after prerequisite chunks — is X's gravity written down, reasoned inside the vocabulary the cut removes. On a cut, existing designs are re-read as quarry and the new approach governs first and foremost: the deletion proceeds on this rule's own sequencing, and a plan that would defer it is evidence about X, never authority over Y. Live specimen, same day as the ruling: the cli_run hollowing plan's chunk ordering was cited to defer that file's deletion minutes before the operator ordered it cut — the plan's area map survives as quarry; its sequencing did not.

Replacement migrations are therefore planned **vertically from the root consumer**, never implemented horizontally by mutating the old representation layer by layer:

1. **Name the authority being replaced.** Identify the exact root consumer — or independently complete consumer population — whose answer currently comes from X. "The parser", "CI" or "v1" is too broad unless the exact consuming operation and subject population are named.
2. **Trace one complete vertical.** Walk from that root through X to determine the minimum representation Y must provide to answer the same real question — consumer-backward design of one complete semantic vertical, never implementation-forward conversion of X's internal layers. (Traversal-order vocabulary is deliberately absent: the durable concepts are semantic closure, the consumer-relative root, the maximal envelope, and branch-and-bound on exact refusal.)
3. **Delete X's production presence as early as you can.** Once X is judged superseded, its production route, machinery, flags and dispatch come out — one deletion, not a sequence — as a declared bounded rung-drop; git history keeps the corpse as quarry and oracle, and what deletion removes is X's authority and its gravity. Two carve-outs bound "as you can": a boundary where the coverage gap is unacceptable (outward-facing interfaces, unrollbackable effects, safety floors others depend on) keeps the staged form — Y built in shadow, then one transition that switches the root and deletes X together; and delete-first presupposes a minimal Y able to hold the boundary *immediately*, even under-covering — where no Y can hold it at all (the v1 seed before any candidate image exists), X stays but stays **frozen**: no new investment, no completion work, quarry-in-place, because the attractor cost accrues either way — and frozen includes X's *growth surfaces*: a host-builtin registry, an escape-hatch arm, a compatibility table inside a frozen X accepts no new rows, because each row is a deferred modeling obligation the surface's existence recruited (the 2026-08-15 receipt: 38 of the interpreter's 125 host builtins had accreted into one driver file precisely because the surface accepted them), and a freeze that still accepts rows is not a freeze.
4. **Fix forward minimally with the new solution.** Y starts as the smallest root that takes the boundary — the seed usually already exists — and grows only by earned additions: what breakage proves load-bearing, or what an explicit disposition retains; never "X had it and I am afraid to drop it." The incoming problems are solved **one by one, each from first principles** (operator directive, 2026-08-15 — the general behavior, not a migration special case): every breakage is asked what the broken reference IS in the terminal model — a capability Y should provide (fix Y), a form the terminal model rejects (migrate the corpus, with X as offline oracle), a need that retires with X, or **another weed the error just named, in which case recurse: back up to its root and delete again** — and never restored by reflex to the old binding. And not every error is a load: deletion surfaces two censuses at once — what *depended* on X, and what X was *hiding* (an absorbing structure masks its deficits, §5, so their frequency was zero by construction until the mask came off; the import strip uncovered a declaration-registration gap that was never a load at all, and the floor deletion uncovered whole-tree defects the old path had absorbed). A load gets a disposition; an uncovered defect gets a root-cause fix in Y — it was always broken, X merely made it unobservable. Bulk restoration under a red tree is the attractor's last trick: each fix made "the fast way" is the old way, and X's decomposition rebuilds itself inside Y one convenience at a time. Work at class grain, not occurrence grain — one rule per diagnostic class, applied mechanically within it — but decide each class deliberately and in sequence. From its first increment Y has one executing root, one exact subject, a typed outcome and a discriminating RED, and every admitted component survives substantially unchanged into terminal Y — meaning its semantic authority and terminal consumer survive; its internals may be refined. X may serve from history or a pinned ref as an external differential oracle over the same subject; Y never calls X, falls back to X, or derives its answer from X — agreement is an observation about two independent answers, never an implementation technique.
5. **Close the gap against green, never against X.** The rung-drop's restoration trigger is the tree green again — every enrolled test and gate passing through Y — plus explicit restoration or retirement of the declared silent residue deletion could not make break. Output-parity with X is never the bar: parity against X's shape re-imports X's decomposition. For the staged exception, admission is the identity join over dispositioned obligations, and the old route dies in the same PR that completes the join.
6. **Keep the evidence for surviving claims, not the obsolete machinery — and not evidence whose only subject is retired.** Discriminating witnesses that establish an obligation Y still claims stay enrolled against Y; production machinery whose only purpose was to implement or compare X is deleted; and a witness whose only subject is behavior Y deliberately rejects is retired *with a receipt*, never left standing — otherwise tests become the mechanism by which obsolete behavior grants itself immortality. This is consistent with §4b's dissolution-on-climb rule and distinct from it: a climb keeps the RED because the claim survives at a higher rung; a rejection retires the claim itself, and its evidence follows it.

**A replacement root is consumer-relative, and supersession precedes translation.** A root is not a module or a top-level node; it is `exact consumer × exact subject population × exact semantic contract`. Before assigning descendant migration work, enumerate every *production* consumer of the old structure and map each exact consumer contract to a replacement root. When a replacement root satisfies an old root's complete contract — every admission and every refusal — and intercepts every remaining production path into its descendants, **those descendants are a deletion population, not migration subjects, and no corresponding Y representation is owed.** If `a → b → c → d` and `b1` satisfies the whole contract formerly produced by `b`, `c` and `d`, then `c1` and `d1` should not exist; building them is work on representations Y makes irrelevant. A descendant reached by any consumer *outside* that superseded path remains an independent root and is analyzed recursively — the analysis is a consumer-relative dominator question, and the deletion is licensed only when the new root dominates every remaining production path into the subtree. Evidence describing a superseded descendant's required behavior survives and is re-enrolled against the replacement even when the descendant's production representation is deleted. The reviewer's question, applied to every artifact a migration proposes: **will this be consumed substantially unchanged by terminal Y, or is a higher replacement root going to make it disappear?** If the latter, it belongs branch-locally as an experiment, in the quarry as evidence, or in the deletion census — never on the production roadmap.

**And the census runs over dispositions, not raw edges: currently consumed ≠ required to survive — a consumer must earn survival.** Most of X's internal consumer edges are consequences of X's chosen decomposition; they say how X works, not what the future system requires. Things built *above* X may exist only to compensate for it — adapters, repair passes, fallback dispatchers, special diagnostics, orchestration workarounds — and if Y makes the underlying behavior unnecessary they are deletion population, not load. Before the cut is computed, every consumer-relative obligation is dispositioned against the terminal design — retained under the same contract, retained under a changed terminal contract, replaced by a named Y authority, reduced to evidence for a surviving claim, or retired with a reason — each retention naming the authority that justifies it: a surviving external interface, a surviving invariant, stored-artifact compatibility, an independently useful terminal consumer, a deliberate transition or rollback commitment. "Something currently calls it" is not a justification: **reliance establishes migration cost, never correctness.** The admission condition is two zeros, not one — `undispositioned consumers = 0` AND `uncovered surviving consumers = 0`: an undecided consumer blocks the cut but is never silently treated as retained, and a retired consumer needs no coverage at all. Three different things, never conflated: a **surviving obligation** (Y must cover it), **existing reliance** (measured and dispositioned), and **obsolete behavior** (Y should make it impossible) — confusing the second with the first is how compatibility layers become permanent. For an internal boundary under delete-first this census is not a prerequisite ledger — the deletion performs it (what refuses is what was load-bearing) and the dispositions are written by the fixes; the up-front form is owed only where deletion cannot be the instrument: gap-intolerant boundaries, frozen X, and the silent residue the substrate cannot make loud. The planning carrier is `gunbc.replacement_cut` (`ReplacementCut`, `ConsumerDisposition`, `admit_replacement_cut_plan`). The row cannot be authored without answering why the boundary is not higher (`HigherBoundaryAnswer` — the descended arm names its parent by `ReplacementCutId`, a joinable typed reference, never prose alone); the boundary census is explicit about emptiness (`BoundaryCensus`: enumerated-but-empty refuses as the ⊥-conflation, a genuinely consumer-free boundary declares itself with a basis); X's residual roles come from the closed `ExternalXRole` vocabulary — a forbidden role (fallback, internal resolver, production second opinion) has no constructor, and no-oracle-needed is a justified arm, never an ambiguous empty list; evidence is dispositioned on the same one axis (`EvidenceDisposition`: re-enroll, replace, retire with its reason, or `EvidenceUndecided` — visible parking that blocks admission exactly like a consumer's `Undecided`, since a deliberate deferral is still not a disposition against the terminal design). Plan admission refuses — typed, per-row, all causes counted — on any undecided row, uncovered surviving consumer, coverage or replacement naming the old root itself, duplicate consumer/evidence/region member, identical old and new roots, self-referential descent, an enumerated-but-empty external-role list, or empty covered region; and the verdict is sealed and bound — `AdmittedReplacementCutPlan` is a `sole_constructor` record whose one in-module mint is `admit_callers`-sealed to the admission fold alone, carrying the cut identity and a criteria digest over every plan field (`admitted_matches_plan` recomputes), so a forged or plan-detached admitted verdict is unwritable rather than merely discouraged. What plan admission deliberately does not establish — the authored census joined against an independently *observed* consumer population, obligation-grain coverage, executed parent-refusal receipts — is the migration-cut authority program; **no roadmap node is structurally registered to a cut yet**, and the typed roadmap projection is that program's future obligation, not a present fact.

"Divorced from X" means **Y is semantically independent of X's authority**, not that Y rewrites stable primitives X happens to use. Y may freely reuse tokenization, parsing, `Node`/`Edge`, occurrence identity, the exact source inventory, and target-independent evaluation; X may serve — from history or a pinned ref once its production presence is deleted, or live only under the frozen-X and staged carve-outs — as a bootstrap producer, an external differential oracle, a mechanical corpus rewriter, or a historical measurement source. X may **not** be Y's fallback for unsupported cases, internal resolver, implicit provider, second opinion whose answer controls production, or a hidden implementation behind a Y-shaped carrier. And the minimum Y is not the smallest representation that executes the happy path: **it must preserve every required refusal** — a faster implementation that permits a binding the old one refused has erased a correctness distinction rather than completed Y.

**"Atomic" describes the authority transition, not the amount of implementation work or the number of commits.** It does not mean one commit, one PR, no incremental development, or migrating the repository at once. It means Y has one coherent root; ordinary production never accumulates consumers that understand both X and Y; and X's authority ends in one motion. Under the delete-first default there is no shadow interval at all — the deletion is the transition, minimal Y takes the boundary immediately, and the declared bounded gap replaces the dual-authority overlap, trading visible temporary under-coverage for the overlap's invisible costs: two authorities, attention capture, shadow periods that empirically never end. The SCM vehicle is an **integration branch** (operator-directed 2026-08-15): a breaking cut never lands on main mid-loop — fork from main at a recorded base, make the root deletion the branch's first commit, and run the fix-forward loop there, where the tree is legitimately red on main's terms; main receives the cutover as one merge when the branch meets Y's green bar, so main never hosts a dual-authority interval and never sees the red. Work inside the region targets the branch, not main, and the branch inherits the freeze — it exists to build Y against the deletion's censuses, never as a third place to keep X alive. Where the staged exception applies, Y is exercised through a real shadow consumer while being built, and one accepted transition moves the root and deletes X's route in the same motion. When one cutover would be too large, partition by an **independently complete root consumer or cohort, never by internal layer**. A cohort is complete only when its exact identities execute through Y, are physically absent from X's schedule or dispatch, and have no fallback: migrating one exact witness population and deleting its old route can be a cohort; converting the lexer, then the parser, then the normalizer while ordinary execution still enters through the old root is the painful form. The cohort axis partitions subjects; the depth axis is governed by a **greedy root-first search: attempt the maximal cut, and descend only on an exact refusal.** The standing question is "why can't we cut at the root — and why not higher", never "which deep module can we swap first": the deletion envelope expands *downward* through X's implementation and *upward* through consumers, adapters, repair passes and workarounds whose only justification was X, stopping only at a contract with an independent reason to survive. There is no conservation-of-effort argument for defaulting deep: a separate production transfer at `c0 → c1` beneath a still-old `b0` pays admission surfaces, receipts, rollback boundaries and X-compatibility work the root cut never pays, so the maximal cut is usually the *cheapest* semantic plan even when Y's implementation work inside it is large. A refusal of the maximal cut is the only license to descend, and the refusal names where: **Y-incomplete** — fix Y and retry the same cut; the missing behavior belongs in terminal Y, not at a lower migration boundary; **an escaping consumer** — a live edge into the region from outside it; disposition that consumer: redirect it to a Y root, enlarge the cut set to replace it, or explicitly retire it; or **opaque** — bisect to *localize* the blocking obligation; bisection is the diagnostic fallback for a refusal that cannot name itself, never the migration order. A deep cut is legitimate exactly as the fallback decomposition of a refused root cut, under conditions: the lower boundary corresponds to an independently meaningful terminal-Y contract, carries every load crossing its region with no X-only adapter (an adapter needed to make the swap fit is the tell the boundary was not shared), immediately deletes a nonempty X region, and survives terminal Y unchanged — the lexer-then-parser ladder executed as complete swap-and-delete at such seams is this rule's staged *fallback*, never its default. The graph object is a minimum semantic cut over admitted surviving obligations, searched branch-and-bound: propose the maximal region, prove or refuse it, split only the refused portion, prune everything covered.

Selective loading, affected sets, caches, batching, aliases and short names are **downstream projections of a correct Y, not prerequisites for establishing its semantics**. The first replacement deliberately chooses the simplest complete form — often the whole subject, canonical names, one manifest, one fold — and derives the smaller or faster projections afterward. A PR that introduces a new X↔Y adapter, production feature flag, dual-dispatch arm or fallback is introducing active migration debt, and per §5 that requires an explicit operator verdict on the exact introduced population; branch-local experiment remains unrestricted so long as the disposable machinery is deleted before merge.

Every replacement migration names, at exact identity grain: the old root consumer and subject population; the new shadow root; the independent outer oracle; the cutover consumer; the exact production machinery deleted at cutover; and the discriminating evidence retained afterward. Its terminal receipt establishes:

```
new root consumers                  = exact declared population
old root consumers                  = 0
undispositioned consumers           = 0
uncovered surviving consumers       = 0
production X<->Y adapters           = 0
production fallbacks                = 0
migration feature flags             = 0
unreconciled subject identities     = 0
retained discriminating evidence    = exact declared population
retired consumers and evidence      = exact declared population, each with a receipt
```

This rule does not prohibit incremental **refinement** — a change whose result remains the same single authority, survives the terminal architecture, and never requires a consumer to understand both representations. The test is not the size of the diff; it is whether the edit reduces the number of authorities or creates another one that must later be removed. **Receipts, in both directions.** The top-cutting shape, as read at the 2026-08-15 replanning review — dated measurements of then-open, still-moving branches, kept as the receipt of that reading and never as live status: gunbc#8080 fused native test execution, corpus migration and self-hosting while the displacement numerator stayed at 0/8,876 and the branch closed; gunbc#8115 had accumulated namespace machinery across 52 commits and >10,000 added lines while ordinary compilation still consumed the import-era path, the authored import population having grown to 18,628 lines across 2,884 files; gunbc#8244 had deleted the entry-major execution model but was fitting the new preparation concept into the old coordinator, plan, batch and policy structure across 88 commits and 250 files, with three plan-driven callers still demanding the subject separately. Both branches were BANKRUPTED on 2026-08-15 — frozen as quarry with their exact-identity machinery and controls retained as evidence, one having grown to the point of adding 351 import lines to make the import era internally complete — and recut as delete-first programs: the floor by deleting the CI step outright and re-adding obligations one by one from first principles, the imports by stripping every authored import line (19,173 across 2,955 files in the scratch measurement) and working the resulting typed diagnostics forward class by class. The careful branches died; their quarry survives. The root-cut shape: gunbc#8146 did not migrate 740 non-executing Rust tests one at a time — it selected the eleven-module executing kernel, deleted the rest, changed the authority from "classify every departure" to "declare every resident", left no compile-only fallback, and turned ~24,000 deleted lines into 56 tests that actually execute; gunbc#8182 removed one dead semantic family end to end across the CLI, primitive registry, generated dispatch and interpreter rather than deleting one layer and leaving adapters behind. And the delete-first ruling's own receipt is the 2026-08-15 required-floor session: successive agents were captured by the old floor's structure — roster unions, test relocation, instrumentation of machinery already judged ill-conceived — until the step was ordered deleted outright, obligations re-added one by one as breakage and dispositions earned them.

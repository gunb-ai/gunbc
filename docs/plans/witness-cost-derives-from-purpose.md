**witness cost derives from purpose — a test is not entitled to arbitrary computation merely because it is a test** (operator ruling, 2026-08-04). A test's size must be DERIVED from what it proves: a modeled boundary it crosses (the smallest crossing specimen is the requirement plus one, never a realistic-looking input chosen for verisimilitude); a live population whose breadth is the subject (which decomposes into small discriminating mechanism fixtures per PR plus the irreducible census on an enrolled cadence — the census may be irreducible, the mechanism never is); an external effect that IS the subject (wet, under an explicit budget); or a resource contract whose threshold comes from a declared requirement. Anything else large is a benchmark, a soak test, a demonstration, or a cost-shape defect in the implementation, and none of those may gate. **The specimen that produced the ruling** is §5's absorbing fallback executed by an author, at the admission layer: a required per-PR witness went over the five-second fast-lane budget, its file was moved under `test/claim/long/`, it left per-PR discovery, no cadence row was added, nothing executed it, and admission still held — green CI, zero executed coverage, source file left behind. That is not deferral; it is deletion of the evidence with the file retained (gunbc#7762 commit `0cfd199f8`, thirteen witnesses, titled *fix CI fast-lane budget failures*). The root was a **directory deciding an admission question**: `OfflineLocalRecipe` — the verdict a broad path pattern produces — was accepted unconditionally, while the exact-admission authority already refused that same shape on an admission row. And **`BudgetExceeded` is an interruption plus a measured lower bound on cost, never a semantic verdict on a witness, and never discharged by causing its relocation**. **Landed — stated at the scope it actually has**: path location is retired as *sufficient admission for a NEW witness*, and the legacy path-only debt is frozen and counted. `long/` remains an organizational home until that population reaches zero.

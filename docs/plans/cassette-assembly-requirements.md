# Cassette assembly convergence

**Goal:** derive an accepted physical assembly from one selected design, its outstanding requirements and independently observed inventory. [Inventory implementation](cassette-inventory-convergence-implementation.md) records the shadow operations; selection, owned interfaces/routes and production cutover remain open. A new report alone does not complete this migration.

**Scope:** finish the unpowered fit prototype without a standoff retrofit. External connections default to one Ethernet and one protected DC branch; BMC Ethernet is optional, and the existing power-feed model determines board-side plugs. Count internal fan/PWM/cooler wiring. On-board SSDs need clearance, retention, heat and mass assessment, not external drive cables. Resting a board on the tray cannot admit powered operation.

**Method:** candidate production → `std.decision` selection receipt → fixed versioned desired assembly/demand census → goal-blind observation → `std.goal_assessment` → remedy selection → guarded work → independent inspection/readback → reassessment. Selection owns board/fan/cooler, connections, routes, inlet and orientation; a Pareto front or passing checks cannot silently choose policy. New evidence reopens selection and versions the goal.

Observers receive subjects and coverage requests, not desired answers; unread inventory is not empty. Model/CAD/toolpath evidence may establish scoped design claims, while installed position and physical acceptance need attributable observations. Preserve known deviations beside unknowns, and distinguish invalid comparisons from failed observation. Refuse dependent work with missing prerequisites while allowing independently admitted work.

**Owners:** retain `assembly_geometry` identities/frames and interface relationships; map every `cassette_profile.DesignObligation` and `server_power_interface.InterfaceObligation` to an owned typed demand or explicit open item. Reuse `pairing_screen`, `node_power_feed`, `exhaust_thermal`, `process_geometry` and `deposited_geometry`. Derive `harness_census` from selected connections/routes. `bore_has_mate`'s infinite-axis/diameter check is only a baseline. Neither fabric provider policy nor compiler-effect resource enums are physical interface semantics.

**Shared structure:** component identity/revision/placement; owned ports with transformed frames and contracts; lifecycle-scoped demands; explicitly selected bindings and quantities; one desired assembly; coverage/source/time-bound observations; existing assessment results with located evidence and closure triggers. Preserve provisional coordinates as observations, never fabricated vendor facts. Index owners/ports once, account for shared capacity, then evaluate local and cross-part constraints. Candidate search stays separate. Duplicate/missing identities, ambiguous assignments, occupied exclusive ports and overdraw refuse explicitly; input order cannot change findings. Exploded-view offsets never enter engineering checks.

| Domain | Obligations that must survive modeling |
|---|---|
| Mechanical | Real mate identity, roles, axes/tolerances, finite engagement/grip, bearing/contact permissions; geometry does not prove strength. |
| Electrical/network | Family/keying/gender, pinout/rail/polarity, signal/protocol, protected branch and shared current; one DC branch may feed several board plugs. |
| Routing | Named endpoints, connector/boot envelopes, section/diameter, bend limit, retention/strain relief, insertion/latch access, service slack; sweep against solids, rotors and other routes with only declared contact exceptions. Cut lengths need path, termination and slack evidence. |
| Airflow | Placed inlet/outlet/direction, rotor exclusion and obstruction; installed pressure/flow, temperatures and fan failure stay separate from free-air ratings. |
| Fabrication | Instance/process/orientation, supports and bed; preserve off-plane refusal until a qualified producer exists. Geometry cannot select retraction or prove cooling/stability. |
| Service | Installed and removal configurations, named disconnects and accessible latches/slack; static fit cannot prove middle-stack removal. |

**First route candidates:** rear Ethernet escapes around the cradle to a retained side route; protected DC follows an edge to front board inputs; rear fan wiring reaches a compatible front header; cooler leads avoid rotors. Select side/inlet with evidence, not this prose. The proposed I/O reservation is not a connector/bend/access model; unresolved coordinates remain open.

**Work identity:** derive required slots and multiplicity from manufacturable component occurrences. Purchasing, routing, assembly and inspection are not all print jobs. Reserve claimed/running/uncertain/completed-awaiting-inspection instances; they need reconciliation, not duplicates. Separate goal slot, revision, physical item, plate and attempt identities; reject stale goal/baseline plans. Preserve accepted siblings and old claims across revisions. New rejection affects only its slots. Atomic multi-instance reservation, controller hold, signed bed-clear and durable start claims remain mandatory; hashing a new plate is not idempotency. `FINISH` means awaiting inspection, never acceptance. Inspect the exact subject independently of command success; never replay uncertainty.

`std.goal_assessment` owns no effectful cycle. The shared cycle must first land with `gunbc.fleet.fleet_converge_plan` as consuming integration; cassette binds it afterward, retaining `printer_actuation` as sole actuator. Do not invent a cassette-local generic cycle or treat `std.realization_reconcile` as evidence that observation happened.

**Remaining sequence:**

- CAS-1/2: validate current-head CI, then make the shared solid generator consume `bed_partition`/`tray_panel_features` while preserving exact r06 Altra geometry. Support Altra, GH200, Collins and Jade profiles without substituting missing outlines/mounts. Minimum axis-grid allocation is not unrestricted nesting or completed large-board CAD.
- CAS-3/4: select the largest fitting standard rear fan from one contiguous opening, using vendor hole patterns and actual depth/service/bed bounds. Complete removable guarded mounts and cassette-owned corner joinery. Different profiles need compatible interfaces/load paths; a middle removal requires support above until an alternate load path is modeled. Guard openings, clip fit and collision-free solids do not prove finger protection, retention or load capacity.
- CAS-5/6: place modular single-PSU-default power, protected branches and actual harnesses. Fold complete placed masses, cooler transport loads and hollow/jointed support geometry into reactions, buckling, tipping, shear and creep. Shoe bearing area is not post wall area. Qualify installed airflow, fan/header failure and material temperature/load/orientation; CPU temperature or another PETG product cannot qualify this material. No invented measurement or new weighing request.
- CAS-7/8: integrate owner-bound findings, routes, airflow/service space, BOM, quantities and reusable/superseded parts into existing review/preparation roots. Batch planning consumes that same census and durable inventory; physical inspection drives reassessment. Schedule only admitted remaining parts, with fresh start approval. No running print changes or new CI prefix/job are implied.

**Validate:** real assembled/sliced integration plus adverse controls: distant coaxial bores, ambiguous/missing/occupied providers, reversed fan, rotor crossing, impossible bend, blocked latch, wrong pinout, branch/header overload, short lead and unqualified upright grille. Provider changes must move review, census and admission together. Exercise unread inventory, wrong-revision inspection, stale plans, concurrent reservations, restart, uncertain start and partial acceptance. Preserve old checks until consumer cutover; delete old carriers/prose only after every obligation maps. Fit, fabrication and powered-operation goals remain separately admitted.

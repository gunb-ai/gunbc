Collins candidate coordinates therefore remain empty, rather than borrowing Jade's pattern. [Ampere document catalogue](https://amperecomputing.com/customer-connect/products/mt-collins) is the follow-up reference.

The reported dimensions make Collins **32.64 mm deeper and 3.8 mm narrower** than Jade under that axis assignment, not 84 mm longer. Public Collins OCP collateral was not located; this does not prove that none exists. Neither OCP NIC support nor an exhibition proves a published motherboard mechanical specification.

## Jade provisional candidate coordinates

Raster: full PDF page rendered at 2 pixels/point (1224 × 1584), PCB bounds x=394..830, y=594..1083, excluding connector projections. Each axis is scaled independently to the published outline. Coordinates are reproducible planning estimates; submillimetre display precision is not accuracy. No ±1–2 mm accuracy bound has been established, and hole diameters/threads are unknown. A peer review found roughly 0.4–1.7 mm differences in front offsets using different edge picks; treating edge candidates as better than about 2 mm would be unjustified, not a newly established ±2 mm bound. Three further similar symbols were reported near connector-strip ends; they remain unidentified and are not silently added as mounting holes.

## Partition sensitivity and CI scope

With 14 mm margins, Collins is 452.2 mm across against a three-cell capacity of 450 mm (180 mm bed minus 30 mm feature budget per cell). Its fourth column rests on **2.2 mm**; Jade’s rests on **6 mm**. These are planning allocations sensitive to changed source dimensions and design allowances, not robust manufacturing margins. Boundary witnesses check three cells at the exact limit and four at one micrometre over it. Neither allocation certifies standoffs, the T-shaped PCB outline or printable panels.

The broad `test.claim.cassette_` row was removed from `required_gate_prefixes` at the operator’s request. It is not replaced by exact required-gate entries. Focused cassette witnesses remain available and are run as scoped validation; the existing changed-witness policy is unchanged.

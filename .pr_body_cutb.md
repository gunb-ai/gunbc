## What this does not establish

The change edits both the `.dag` authority and the hand-written `stage0` mirror, and **only the mirror executes**. The arms establish that the executing binary carries the deletion and that the arms differ in exactly two files.

They did not, on their own, establish that the `.dag` authority is at its regen fixed point — a hand-edited mirror is not a regen receipt. **CI has now supplied that receipt separately: `regen first_generation_equal=true`.** So the mirror edit *is* what the `.dag` authority emits, which rules out a hybrid tree. Note what that predicate does and does not say: it is a fixed point over the *emitter* ("emit again and the bytes do not move"), not a validity predicate over the emitted Rust. It rules out the hybrid class; it does not certify that the generated tree compiles.
